    Take your hand out of view to add a space. For a double letter (the LL in HELLO), drop your hand briefly between the two. Keys: Space Backspace Enter speaks.

    Teach it your hand

    The app recognises letters by comparing your hand with 2,122 example hands from other people. Adding a few examples of your own hand makes it much more accurate for you. Pick a letter, press Record, and hold the sign for 2 seconds while moving your hand slightly. Only the 21 hand points are stored, in this browser; no images.

    Settings
